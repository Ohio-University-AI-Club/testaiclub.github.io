What we're here to do

Build an inclusive community

Everyone is welcome, from every major and every skill level. We explore AI and the ideas around it, including machine learning and natural language processing.

Get career ready

Meet professionals who deploy AI at scale, learn how the industry works, and make connections that help after graduation.

Share a resource hub

Curated learning materials, project ideas, and up-to-date information on current AI issues and innovations, all in one place.

Meetings

We meet every other Thursday evening. Bring a laptop if you like, but you don't need one. Expect talks, demos, discussion, and time to meet people.

    Project ideas

    Pick one for a weekend, or find teammates at a meeting.

    • Build a chatbot that answers questions about Ohio University's campus resources.
    • Train an image classifier on photos of Athens-area plants or birds.
    • Analyze campus-related text data (with permission) to find trends with NLP.
    • Write a short explainer on a current AI issue and present it at a meeting.
